## Brand & Style

This design system channels the visual language of vintage Japanese theatrical exhibition posters, progressive Tokyo streetwear lookbooks, and high-impact graphic brutalism. Built for an independent anime apparel label, it merges subcultural otaku iconography with elevated graphic design: structured typographic grids, tactile print artifacts, deliberate framing rules, and stark contrasts.

The target audience comprises design-conscious anime enthusiasts, streetwear collectors, and community-driven patrons who value authenticity, limited-edition provenance, and garment craft over mass-market merchandise. The emotional tone evokes anticipation, belonging, warmth, and the tactile thrill of physical ephemera—bringing together the nostalgia of Saturday morning animation with disciplined contemporary print aesthetics.

Visual execution relies on three pillars:
- **Editorial Poster Composition:** Monumental condensed typographic scale juxtaposed against asymmetrical artwork crops, supported by geometric registration marks, katakana running lines, and stamp accents.
- **Graphic Brutalism with Tactile Warmth:** Unapologetic structural grids, crisp monoline division lines, and stark black ink framing balanced by a warm, sunlit ecru paper substrate.
- **High-Fidelity Garment Framing:** Deliberate framing mechanisms (passe-partout borders, museum-grade artwork crops, serial tag badges) designed to elevate illustrated sportswear and screenprints into gallery-grade pieces.

## Colors

The palette derives from physical Japanese woodblock prints and archival screenprinted apparel, set over an organic newsprint or unbleached cotton background rather than sterile digital white.

- **Primary (`#AD3F32` - Vermilion Torii):** A traditional Japanese cinnabar/vermilion red. Reserved for high-priority actions, countdown markers, production alerts, campaign milestones, and official seal/hanko stamps.
- **Secondary (`#387D75` - Spirit Teal / `#3A4E48` - Muted Sage):** Drawn directly from the bathhouse water accents and river dragon scales. Serves as functional indicators, progress states, secondary banners, and environmental badges.
- **Tertiary (`#9F87AF` - Soft Wisteria):** An ethereal purple derived from vintage obi cords and spirit realm accents. Used for mascot narrative cards, subtle highlight pills, and tag accents.
- **Neutral (`#0A0908` - Obsidian Ink):** Dense sumi ink black. Forms the structural skeleton, brutalist bounding borders, heavy display typography, and high-contrast frames.
- **Canvas (`#FBF7EE` & `#EEE5BC`):** Warm ecru and unbleached washi surfaces. Interfaces default to `#FBF7EE` for long-form reading comfort, with `#EEE5BC` applied to structural hero sections, modular panels, and sticker backings.
- **Coral Accent (`#F58778`):** Sourced directly from garment piping and jersey stripes, used for active state pings, limited-run callouts, and preorder status trackers.

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary visual architecture of this design system. It operates on two contrasting axes:

1. **The Display Voice (Barlow Condensed):** Tall, commanding, and dense. Display titles are set with tight tracking and uppercase transforms, mirroring the aggressive typesetting of 1970s–90s anime film banners and Tokyo street exhibition posters. All primary headlines, countdown meters, badge labels, and campaign stats utilize this condensed structure.
2. **The Rational Body Voice (Inter):** Neutral, unencumbered, and rigorously legible. Preorder rules, garment specifications, factory milestones, and legal policies require frictionless comprehension. Inter guarantees absolute legibility even on compact mobile views.
3. **Japanese Accents & Subtext:** Accompany hero headings with subtle Japanese katakana or kanji subtitles (e.g., `ヤバイ・ソサエティ` or `千と千尋`) set in small uppercase sizes with generous letter spacing (`0.2em`). Use these as decorative sub-headers, edge margin anchors, or vertical text ribbons along container borders.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured editorial poster rhythm built on a 12-column desktop grid (6-column on tablet, 4-column on mobile) featuring visible or implied framing lines.

- **Hero Framing & Composition:** Hero regions abandon generic centered layouts. They pair an asymmetrical full-height garment graphic crop spanning 7 columns with monumental condensed headlines and campaign counters spanning the remaining 5 columns.
- **Section Margins & Bleeds:** Sections sit inside deliberate outer margins (`2.5rem` desktop, `1rem` mobile), creating a framed "poster board" effect where page backgrounds form an architectural border around the content modules.
- **Grid Dividers:** Sections are partitioned with sharp, visible lines (`1px solid rgba(10, 9, 8, 0.16)` or `2px solid #0A0908`), replacing empty whitespace gaps with deliberate physical grid cells reminiscent of Japanese manga panelling and packaging slips.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12 columns, split-screen asymmetric hero, sticky campaign progress rail, side-by-side garment technical specs.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6 columns, stacked hero with high-impact artwork preview, persistent bottom action bar.
  - **Mobile (≤767px):** 4 columns, tight margins (`1rem`), edge-to-edge artwork preview carousel with `1px` crisp borders, pinned sticky preorder CTA bar above navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, blurred, ambient shadows. Depth is achieved purely through print-making metaphors, physical paper layering, and high-contrast hard offsets:

- **Hard Ink Offset ("Drop Stamp"):** Floating cards, active buttons, and campaign status modules employ a crisp, unblurred drop-shadow offset: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #0A0908`. On press or active states, the offset transitions to `1px 1px 0px #0A0908` with a matching `translate(3px, 3px)`.
- **Tonal Substrates & Surface Stacking:** Surfaces do not float in open air; they rest on distinct paper layers:
  - Base layer: Warm ecru canvas (`#FBF7EE`).
  - Active module panels: Deeper kraft cream (`#EEE5BC`) enclosed in a `1px` or `2px` sumi ink border.
  - Accent cards: Pale wisteria (`#9F87AF` at 15% opacity) or sage wash (`#3A4E48` at 12% opacity).
- **Masking Tape & Stamp Overlays:** Decorative callouts, serial numbered badges, and mascot notes feature hard-angled rotation (`-1.5deg` to `+2deg`) with paper-tape headers (`#E3D8A8`) to evoke physical paste-ups in a design studio.

## Shapes

The shape system strictly uses **sharp, unrounded geometry (`roundedness: 0`)**. 

- **Corners:** `0px` radius on all interactive elements, cards, image frames, and dialogue boxes. This maintains the uncompromising print-editorial aesthetic and echoes traditional woodblock and poster trim lines.
- **Hanko Stamps & Seals:** The sole circular or pill elements on the page are round Japanese signature seals (hanko stamps), circular release number badges, or registered trademark symbols (`©`), which deliberately disrupt the rigid rectangular grid.
- **Notched & Chamfered Accents:** Limited-edition tags, size charts, and coupon/preorder vouchers may incorporate inverted 45-degree corner cutouts (chamfers) or ticket-stub notches to reinforce apparel manufacturing and physical garment tickets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Preorder CTA):** Sharp rectangular shape, filled with vermilion red (`#AD3F32`), text in Barlow Condensed bold uppercase in ecru (`#FBF7EE`), surrounded by a `2px solid #0A0908` border and a `4px 4px 0px #0A0908` hard drop shadow. Active state shifts by `2px` diagonally and reduces shadow to `2px`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with `#0A0908` text, `1.5px solid #0A0908` border, hovering into an inverted fill of `#0A0908` with `#FBF7EE` text.
- **Sticky Mobile Bar:** Anchors to viewport bottom, displaying remaining goal percentage, price, and a full-width primary button.

### Badges, Stamps & Seals
- **Campaign State Pill:** Monospaced or condensed bold text inside a `1px solid #0A0908` bounding box. `OPEN` wears a vibrant teal background (`#387D75`); `GOAL REACHED` wears vermilion (`#AD3F32`); `ARCHIVE` wears obsidian (`#0A0908`).
- **Hanko Seal (Approved Tag / Mascot Note):** A red square or circular stamp with a rough `1.5px` border containing vertical katakana or Yuu-chan icon, tilted by `4deg`.

### Progress & Threshold Tracker
- **Funding Goal Bar:** A brutalist physical meter. A `2px solid #0A0908` frame over `#EEE5BC` filled with solid `#387D75` or `#AD3F32`. Shows both jersey count numerator/denominator (e.g. `48 / 67 JERSEYS BACKED`) alongside percentage and timestamp. The bar includes visible tick marks at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%.

### Cards & Apparel Framing
- **Garment Frame:** High-resolution artwork and jersey mockups are housed inside museum-style frames featuring double-line borders (`2px solid #0A0908` outer, `1px` hairline inner) on `#FBF7EE` or `#EEE5BC` backing.
- **Information Cards:** White/ecru surface bounded by `1px solid rgba(10, 9, 8, 0.2)` with a solid black header banner for category titles (e.g., `SPECIFICATION [01]`, `TIMELINE // 2026`).

### Form Inputs & Size Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Crisp rectangles with `#0A0908` borders, transparent backgrounds on cream, and placeholder text in `caption-mono` style. Focused fields show an extra `2px` black border and an accent indicator.
- **Size Chips (XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL):** Grid of square boxes with `1.5px solid #0A0908` borders. Selected size fills with solid `#0A0908` with `#FBF7EE` text. Out-of-stock sizes display a clean diagonal slash line from bottom-left to top-right.

### Mascot Dialogue Module (Yuu-chan Callout)
- A specialized card styled like an anime production note or director's cut memo: taped header (`#E3D8A8`), soft wisteria/ecru background, Yuu-chan illustration resting on the bottom-right corner, explaining the preorder model and refund guarantees in warm, human language.
